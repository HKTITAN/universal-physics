## 1. What islands/replica wormholes are now agreed to establish

`[INFERENCE, within controlled settings]` The QES/island formula, justified by replica-wormhole saddles of the gravitational path integral, computes the fine-grained radiation entropy and returns a unitary Page curve: Penington (arXiv:1905.08255, JHEP 09 (2020) 002); AEMM (arXiv:1905.08762, JHEP 12 (2019) 063); PSSY (arXiv:1911.11977, JHEP 03 (2022) 205); AHMST (arXiv:1911.12333, JHEP 05 (2020) 013). Consensus reading: Hawking omitted saddles, not physics beyond the semiclassical regime.

**Not established** `[OPEN]`: the mechanism of information escape; the interior encoding (leading proposal: non-isometric codes protected by complexity — AEHPV, arXiv:2207.06536 `[SPECULATIVE]`); a controlled derivation for a realistic asymptotically-flat, dynamically-formed 4D black hole; higher-dimensional replica saddles beyond braneworld constructions.

## 2. Standing objection 1 — ensemble vs. single theory `[CONTESTED]`

- PSSY read replica wormholes as an implicit **ensemble average**; Marolf–Maxfield formalize baby-universe α-sectors with exact null-state degeneracies (arXiv:2002.08950, JHEP 08 (2020) 044).
- Contra genericity: Schlenker–Witten — connected geometries never contribute to **sub-black-hole-threshold** observables — proven in 3d via renormalized-volume methods; ensemble-nonexistence argued in higher-d examples (arXiv:2202.01372, JHEP 07 (2022) 143); apparent averaging traced to black-hole chaos.
- 2025–2026 continuation: the OP-50 closed-universe debate (Harlow–Usatyuk–Zhao arXiv:2501.02359 vs Engelhardt–Gesteau arXiv:2504.14586, Antonini et al. arXiv:2507.10649) and the Kudler-Flam–Witten fixed-$N$ non-convergence / averaging-over-$N$ line (arXiv:2510.06376, 2605.15180) — both already registered in the wiki (OP-50; OP-44 fourth option). **No theorem either way** for a single unitary theory underlying island entropies.

## 3. Standing objection 2 — massive graviton / gravitating bath `[CONTESTED]`

*(The orchestrator brief's "massive gravitino" appears to be a slip: no gravitino-based objection was locatable; the literature objection is the massive gravitON.)*

- All controlled $d>2$ island constructions (Karch–Randall brane + non-gravitating bath) have a **massive graviton**; island contributions vanish in the massless limit (Geng–Karch, arXiv:2006.02438, JHEP 09 (2020) 121).
- Islands as entanglement wedges disconnected from the asymptotic region strain the **gravitational Gauss law** in long-range massless gravity (GKPRRRS, arXiv:2107.03390, JHEP 01 (2022) 182); with a **gravitating bath** the radiation entropy becomes time-independent (arXiv:2012.04671, SciPost Phys. 10, 103 (2021)).
- 2023–2026: string-embedded islands with parametrically light (nonzero-mass) gravitons inside massive-gravity EFT validity (Demulder–Gnecchi–Lavdas–Lüst, arXiv:2204.03669, JHEP 02 (2023) 016); Gauss-law argument extended to low dimensions incl. JT (Geng, arXiv:2312.13336, SciPost Phys. 19, 146 (2025)); island formula derived via replica wormholes **on the KR brane** (Geng, arXiv:2405.14872, JHEP 01 (2025) 063); consolidated position + proposal of a new entanglement-wedge notion for massless gravity (Geng, arXiv:2509.22775).
- Opposing camp: Raju et al. **holography of information** (review arXiv:2012.05770 [unverified journal-ref: Phys. Rept. (2022)]; wiki A2 cluster) — in massless gravity boundary data is complete, islands unnecessary. Its Dec-2025 refinement (GJST arXiv:2512.18912; FINDINGS iteration 11) has *dressing re-localizing information*. **Convergence:** both camps now locate the deciding structure in the gravitational dressing; they disagree on what it implies.

## 4. The von Neumann-algebraic turn — proven vs conjectured

**Proven (mathematics; physics status = perturbative/strict large-$N$):**
1. Type III$_1\to$II$_\infty$ crossed product; BH entropy defined up to an additive constant (Witten, arXiv:2112.12828, JHEP 10 (2022) 008).
2. dS static patch + observer $\Rightarrow$ Type II$_1$; vN entropy $=S_{\rm gen}$; max-entropy state (CLPW, arXiv:2206.10780, JHEP 02 (2023) 082). **The crossed product installs the observer** — without one, no nontrivial gauge-invariant static-patch observables.
3. Microcanonical large-$N$ algebra II$_\infty$; replica-free special case of QES; a version of the GSL from entropy monotonicity (CPW, arXiv:2209.10454, JHEP 04 (2023) 009).
4. Arbitrary Killing horizons incl. Kerr and collapse (KLS I, arXiv:2309.15897, PRD 111, 025013); general subregions (JSS, arXiv:2306.01837, JHEP (2023) — wiki-verified coordinates JHEP 12 (2023) 020); physical clocks / cosmology (arXiv:2406.02116, 2406.01669 — wiki-verified iter 6); complete constraint group + edge modes (KLS II, arXiv:2601.07910); per-cut subregion algebras, GSL for non-stationary linearized perturbations, QFC **proven within the perturbative regime** (Chandrasekaran–Flanagan, arXiv:2601.07915).
5. Background-independent worldline-algebra proposal (Witten, arXiv:2308.03663, no journal ref).

**Conjectured / open:** any nonperturbative statement (OP-44's $N_\ast$, theorem-free both sides — unchanged); fixing the additive constant / deriving the $A/4$ count; removing the installed observer/clock/corner-symmetry input (A-CP-KILLING pattern — unchanged).
