## 2. The near-miss, and a sharpened watch target — Marrakchi's bicentralizer-flow theorem

**Marrakchi, *Ergodicity of the bicentralizer flow and Kadison's problem*, arXiv:2606.23636 (v1 2026-06-22, math.OA, 12 pp., un-refereed).** Abstract verbatim: "We show that the relative bicentralizer flow of a type III₁ irreducible subfactor with expectation is always ergodic. As a consequence, every irreducible subfactor with expectation in a factor with separable predual contains a maximal abelian subalgebra. This completes the solution to Kadison's problem on maximal abelian subalgebras from 1967." `[ESTABLISHED as preprint claim]`

Three independent referees each verified the citation live and each downgraded strong→weak on the same grounds:

- **It is not bicentralizer triviality.** Connes' bicentralizer problem (is B(M,φ) = ℂ1 for every type III₁ factor?) remains OPEN in general — the theorem establishes ergodicity of the *flow*, a different (weaker-in-the-relevant-direction) statement. `[ESTABLISHED]`
- **It is not a carrier and installs nothing.** Pure subfactor theory: no localization net, no causal order, no Krein/metric datum. Its output — a maximal abelian subalgebra — is abelian/definite: structurally the *opposite* of a localized indefinite pairing. No standing closure is evaded. `[INFERENCE, high]`
- **The transfer-blocker is the hypothesis "with expectation."** The iter-12 physics-net bicentralizer lever ([notes/2026-06-19-iter12-physics-net-bicentralizer-lever.md](2026-06-19-iter12-physics-net-bicentralizer-lever.md)) already recorded that the physics local inclusions M(O) ⊂ M(W) are irreducible with **no normal conditional expectation** (non-discrete, infinite index) — precisely the hypothesis this theorem needs. The result therefore does not touch the physics-net case. `[ESTABLISHED per iter-12 record]`

**Why it still matters (the watch's actual product):** the cluster around the wiki's named-adjacent problem is *visibly active* — Marrakchi's conditional Inventiones-track result (arXiv:2308.15163), the Houdayer–Marrakchi selflessness equivalence (arXiv:2511.11409), now an unconditional ergodicity theorem, all by the field's central authors. **Sharpened watch target:** the next genuine signal for clock (1) would be an ergodicity/rigidity theorem for the relative bicentralizer flow of **expectation-free** irreducible inclusions (the physics-net case), or any movement on bicentralizer *triviality* itself. `[INFERENCE — watch-target refinement, no verdict content]`

## 3. The Krein closure absorbs another instance — Bateman–Turok

**Bateman–Turok, *Escape from Ostrogradsky via Hidden Ghost Parity*, arXiv:2607.00096 (v1 2026-06-30, hep-th, 6 pp., un-refereed).** A four-derivative scalar QFT quantized on a Krein space, with a generalized (Krein) Born rule and tree-level positivity of transition probabilities via a "hidden ghost parity" from an O(1,1) two-field embedding. `[ESTABLISHED as preprint claim]`

Referee adjudication (confirmed-weak by one referee, none by two — logged at the ledger grade): a textbook instance of the **iter-12 Krein-route closure, channel (B) installed-not-derived** — with one instructive correction to the naive smuggle-reading. The indefinite pairing itself is *GNS-induced* from (algebra, state) — the Wightman two-point function W̃(p) = θ(p⁰)δ′(p²) has an indefinite derivative-of-delta structure, so the inner product's indefiniteness is not postulated. What IS installed by hand: **n₁** (the covariant commutator is fixed on background Minkowski — the causal order is the pre-given lightcone), and the **global, non-unique ghost-parity κ** (the Krein fundamental symmetry, from the hand-picked O(1,1) embedding; the paper itself: "no preferred choice of ghost parity" — no naturality, no localization, no Aut(N,Ω)-equivariance). It evades no standing closure. `[INFERENCE, high]`

**Ledger value:** (i) one more independent instance of the closure pattern — each new Krein-QFT that must install κ by hand is corroborating evidence for the screen's reach; (ii) the Krein Born rule + tree-level-positivity machinery is genuinely useful *background* for any future carrier constructor (it answers "could an indefinite carrier be probabilistically consistent?" affirmatively — the open problem was never consistency, it is naturality). `[INFERENCE]`
