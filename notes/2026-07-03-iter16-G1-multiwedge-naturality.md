## 1. The question (the exact open object)

The dossier's residual, verbatim in structure: modular naturality forces a carrier $\eta$ into the commutant $\{\Delta_O^{it}\}'$ **in $B(H)$**, not into the abelian $W^*(\Delta_O)$; for type III$_1$ the modular spectrum is degenerate so these differ. The open question: *is every algebra-compatible ($\mathrm{Ad}\,\eta\in\mathrm{Aut}(M)$), $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal N,\Omega)$-natural, localized carrier in that commutant forced to be a function of $\Delta$ (geometry-void, $\eta_0$-class) or signature-inherited from the pre-installed causal order — or can a genuinely new generated object live there?*

Two housekeeping facts sharpen the target before any new mathematics:

**(Q1) The gap is a multiplicity statement.** `[INFERENCE, high — standard]` For a BW wedge, the Borchers commutation relation $\Delta_W^{it}U(a)\Delta_W^{-it}=U(e^{\mp2\pi t}a)$ (lightlike translations, positive generators) plus Mackey imprimitivity for the $ax{+}b$ group forces $\ln\Delta_W$ to have homogeneous **Lebesgue spectrum on $\Omega^\perp$** — absolutely continuous, spectrum $\mathbb R$, and (for any theory with particle content, e.g. the free scalar field: rapidity line $\otimes$ transverse momenta $\otimes$ Fock layers) **infinite uniform multiplicity**. Hence
$$\{\Delta_W^{it}\}' \;\cong\; L^\infty(\mathbb R)\,\bar\otimes\,B(K),\qquad \dim K=\infty,$$
and $W^*(\Delta_W)\cong L^\infty(\mathbb R)\otimes\mathbb C$ is **very far from maximal abelian** in $\{\Delta_W^{it}\}'$. The "MASA hope" (multiplicity one $\Rightarrow$ commutant $=$ functions of $\Delta$ $\Rightarrow$ gap closes identically) **fails**, definitively. Any closure must control the multiplicity space $K$ by *additional structure*, not by spectral non-degeneracy.

**(Q2) The prior iterations used one wedge.** Verified against the iter-9 and both iter-10 notes: Lemma N1/N2, the centralizer analysis (A2), and the struck ergodicity closure (A1) all work with a **single** modular operator $\Delta$ (one state, one algebra). The naturality *definition* (dossier §1) quantifies over all of $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal N,\Omega)$ — which by BW contains the modular flows of **every** wedge — but no prior track ever intersected the operator-level constraints of two or more wedges. The multi-wedge lever is genuinely new. `[ESTABLISHED — checked against the repo this session]`

One definitional fork governs everything below. The dossier defines naturality by "$\eta_{\alpha x}=\alpha_*\eta_x$ (equivalently $\mathrm{Ad}\,U_\alpha$ commutes with the family $\{\eta_x\}$)". This has two honest readings:

- **(R-global)** the carrier is a *single* form/fundamental symmetry $\eta$ on $H$ (one fiber). Equivariance then degenerates to **invariance**: $[\eta,U_\alpha]=0$ for every $\alpha\in\mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal N,\Omega)$ — in particular $[\eta,\Delta_W^{it}]=0$ for **all** wedges $W$ simultaneously.
- **(R-field)** the carrier is a genuine *field* $\{\eta_x\}_{x\in\mathcal I}$; equivariance constrains each fiber only through the **stabilizer** of its index ($\mathrm{Stab}(W)\supset$ own boost $+$ edge translations $+$ edge rotations; $\mathrm{Stab}(O)\supset$ rotations for a double cone), plus the consistency web across fibers (which is what Lemma N2 exploits).

Both readings are treated; neither is silently favored.

## 2. Lemma G — the formalization

Stated so an operator algebraist can referee it cold.

> **Standing hypotheses (H).** $\mathcal N=\{M(O)\}_{O\in\mathcal I}$ is a local net of von Neumann algebras on a separable Hilbert space $H$, indexed by the double cones and wedges of Minkowski space $\mathbb R^{1,d-1}$ ($d\ge2$), satisfying: isotony; locality; Haag duality; the split property; Poincaré covariance by a strongly continuous positive-energy unitary representation $U$ of $\mathcal P_+^\uparrow$ with **unique vacuum** $\Omega$ ($0$ a simple eigenvalue of the Hamiltonian $H_0$, $U(g)\Omega=\Omega$); Reeh–Schlieder ($\Omega$ cyclic and separating for every $M(O)$, $O$ a double cone or wedge); the **Bisognano–Wichmann property** ($\Delta_W^{it}=U(\Lambda_W(2\pi t))$ for every wedge $W$, where $\Lambda_W$ is the boost preserving $W$); local algebras the hyperfinite type III$_1$ factor.
>
> **Lemma G (net-naturality, operator form).** Let $\eta\in B(H)$ be a self-adjoint unitary ($\eta=\eta^*$, $\eta^2=1$; the fundamental symmetry of a Krein form).
>
> **(G-loc)** If $[\eta,\Delta_W^{it}]=0$ for *some* wedge $W$ and all $t$, and $\eta\in M(O)$ for *some* region $O\subset W$ with $\Omega$ separating for $M(O)$ (in particular any double cone strictly inside $W$, or $W$ itself), then $\eta=\pm1$. **No localized modular-natural carrier exists — indefinite or otherwise.**
>
> **(G-glob)** If $[\eta,\Delta_W^{it}]=0$ for **all** wedges $W$ (reading R-global of naturality) and $\mathrm{Ad}\,\eta(M(W_0))=M(W_0)$ for at least one wedge $W_0$ (algebra-compatibility), then: $[\eta,U(g)]=0$ for all $g\in\mathcal P_+^\uparrow$; $\eta\Omega=\pm\Omega$; $\mathrm{Ad}\,\eta$ preserves *every* $M(O)$ and is an **internal (gauge) symmetry** of the net; the $\pm$-eigenprojections $P_\pm$ of $\eta$ are Poincaré-invariant, so the signature grading carries **no region dependence**: $\Delta_{\rm geo}=0$. Under the split property the symmetry lies in the compact gauge group with its Doplicher–Longo canonical implementation.
>
> **(G-field)** Under reading R-field: every **wedge-indexed fiber** $\eta_W$ (own-flow naturality $[\eta_W,\Delta_W^{it}]=0$ is forced, since $\Delta_W^{it}$ stabilizes the index $W$; localization puts $\eta_W\in M(O)\subsetneq M(W)$ or $\eta_W \in M(W)$) is $\pm1$ by (G-loc). In a **conformal** net the same holds for double-cone fibers ($\Delta_O$ geometric by Hislop–Longo; conformal transport of (G-loc)). The **only** surviving freedom is: double-cone fibers in **massive** nets, where $\Delta_O$ is non-geometric; such a fiber satisfies $\eta_O\in M(O)$, $[\eta_O,\Delta_O^{it}]=0$, i.e. $\eta_O$ lies in the **centralizer of the vacuum on the double-cone algebra**, $M(O)_\omega$. ## 3. The attack, in full detail

### 3.1 The localized horn (G-loc): a three-line theorem

**Step 1 (Fix of the wedge flow is $\mathbb C\Omega$).** Let $\Psi\in H$ with $\Delta_W^{it}\Psi=\Psi$ for all $t$. Let $U_\pm(a)$ be the two lightlike translation groups along the null directions bounding $W$; by BW and Poincaré group theory (pure computation in $\mathcal P_+^\uparrow$),
$$\Delta_W^{it}\,U_\pm(a)\,\Delta_W^{-it}=U_\pm(e^{\mp2\pi t}a).$$
Set $f_\pm(a)=\langle\Psi,U_\pm(a)\Psi\rangle$. Invariance of $\Psi$ gives $f_\pm(a)=f_\pm(e^{\mp2\pi t}a)$ for all $t$: $f_\pm$ is constant on each open half-line. Strong continuity at $a=0$ gives $f_\pm\equiv f_\pm(0)=\|\Psi\|^2$. Equality in Cauchy–Schwarz ($|\langle\Psi,U_\pm(a)\Psi\rangle|=\|\Psi\|\,\|U_\pm(a)\Psi\|$) forces $U_\pm(a)\Psi=\lambda\Psi$ with $\lambda\|\Psi\|^2=\|\Psi\|^2$, so $U_\pm(a)\Psi=\Psi$ for all $a$. The generators satisfy $P_++P_-=2H_0$; hence $H_0\Psi=0$; by uniqueness of the vacuum ($0$ simple), $\Psi\in\mathbb C\Omega$. $\blacksquare$
`[Each ingredient ESTABLISHED: the scaling relation is the geometric BW statement (and holds abstractly by Borchers' theorem, CMP 143 (1992) 315, from the spectrum condition alone); positivity of $P_\pm$ is the spectrum condition; simplicity of $\ker H_0$ is the uniqueness-of-vacuum axiom.]`

**Step 2 (vacuum pinning).** $[\eta,\Delta_W^{it}]=0$ and $\Delta_W^{it}\Omega=\Omega$ give $\Delta_W^{it}(\eta\Omega)=\eta\Omega$; by Step 1, $\eta\Omega=c\Omega$. Since $\|\eta\Omega\|=1$ and $c=\langle\Omega,\eta\Omega\rangle=\overline{\langle\eta\Omega,\Omega\rangle}=\bar c$ (self-adjointness), $c=\pm1$.

**Step 3 (Reeh–Schlieder kill).** $\eta\in M(O)$ and $(\eta-c)\Omega=0$ with $\Omega$ separating for $M(O)$ give $\eta=c1=\pm1$. $\blacksquare$

**Remarks (honesty).**
(i) This subsumes and *slightly extends* the known wedge-centralizer triviality $M(W)_\omega=\mathbb C1$ (the iter-10 "F3 physics fact", referee-upheld): the point missed in iterations 9–10 is that **the localization condition itself confines the carrier to $M(O)\subset M(W)$, where the same two-line ergodicity argument applies** — i.e. $M(O)\cap\{\Delta_W^{it}\}'\subseteq M(W)\cap\{\Delta_W^{it}\}'=M(W)_\omega=\mathbb C1$. The iter-10 A2 referee's corrected Prong-1 *conclusion* ("no localized indefinite carrier exists in the geometric sector") is hereby given a self-contained proof with all hypotheses displayed, and strengthened from "no *indefinite* localized carrier" to "no *non-scalar* localized natural carrier at all."
(ii) The argument nowhere touches the $B(H)$-multiplicity gap of (Q1) — it does not need to. Localization + one wedge's naturality pins the carrier through the *vacuum vector*, bypassing the multiplicity space entirely. This is why the gap (huge as an operator-algebra object) is *inaccessible to localized carriers*.
(iii) Consistency check against standing objects: $\eta_0=\mathrm{sgn}(\ln\Delta_W)$ evades (G-loc) because it is in **no** $M(O)$ (non-localizable — exactly its known defect); the iter-10 centralizer candidates $\mathrm{sgn}(h)$, $h\in M_\omega$, exist only for almost-periodic states, where the BW hypotheses fail — consistent with iter-10 Prong 2. The theorem's boundary matches the known landscape exactly. No standing result is contradicted.

### 3.2 The multi-wedge lever (G-glob): the non-localized sliver is exactly the gauge gradings

This is the genuinely **new** structural result, and the direct answer to the iter-10 A2 referee's flag ("algebra-compatible carriers ($\mathrm{Ad}\,\eta\in\mathrm{Aut}(M)$) that lie in $\{\Delta\}'$ but **not** in $M$ — not fully treated").

**Step 1 (wedge boosts generate the Poincaré group).** `[ESTABLISHED — elementary Lie theory]` Boosts of all orientations generate $L_+^\uparrow$ (products of two non-parallel boosts contain Wigner rotations; polar decomposition closes the argument). Translations arise from boosts of *translated* wedges: with $\Lambda_{W+a}(t)=T(a)\Lambda_W(t)T(-a)$,
$$\Lambda_{W+a}(t)\,\Lambda_W(t)^{-1}=T\big((1-\Lambda_W(t))a\big),$$
and $(1-\Lambda_W(t))$ is invertible on the boost 2-plane (eigenvalues $1-e^{\pm2\pi t}\neq0$), so all translations in the boost plane are reached; varying the wedge orientation reaches all of $\mathbb R^d$. Hence the subgroup of $\mathcal P_+^\uparrow$ generated by $\{\Lambda_W(t): W \text{ wedge},\, t\in\mathbb R\}$ is all of $\mathcal P_+^\uparrow$. Under BW, $\Delta_W^{it}=U(\Lambda_W(2\pi t))$, so
$$[\eta,\Delta_W^{it}]=0\ \forall W,t \quad\Longrightarrow\quad [\eta,U(g)]=0\ \forall g\in\mathcal P_+^\uparrow.$$
(Corroborated structurally by Brunetti–Guido–Longo modular localization, arXiv:math-ph/0203021, where the Poincaré representation is *constructed* from the wedge modular data — see §7.)

**Step 2 (vacuum pinning).** By Step 1 of §3.1 applied to any one wedge, $\eta\Omega=\pm\Omega$; WLOG $\eta\Omega=\Omega$ (else pass to $-\eta$, same form up to overall sign).

**Step 3 (net-compatibility from one wedge).** Assume $\mathrm{Ad}\,\eta(M(W_0))=M(W_0)$. Since $[\eta,U(g)]=0$, for every $g$: $\mathrm{Ad}\,\eta(M(gW_0))=\mathrm{Ad}\,\eta\,\mathrm{Ad}\,U(g)(M(W_0))=\mathrm{Ad}\,U(g)\,\mathrm{Ad}\,\eta(M(W_0))=M(gW_0)$ — all wedges are preserved; by Haag duality $M(O)=\bigcap_{W\supseteq O}M(W)$, all double cones are preserved. So $\gamma:=\mathrm{Ad}\,\eta$ is a net automorphism commuting with the covariance representation and fixing $\Omega$: **an internal (gauge) symmetry** in the standard sense. Under the split property the group of such symmetries is the compact gauge group with the Doplicher–Longo canonical (split/type-I-interpolation) implementation `[ESTABLISHED — Doplicher–Longo, Invent. Math. 75 (1984) 493; pre-arXiv, canonical]`; this identifies $\eta$ (given Step 2's vacuum fixing) with the canonical implementer of an involutive gauge symmetry.

**Step 4 (geometry audit: $\Delta_{\rm geo}=0$).** The spectral projections $P_\pm=(1\pm\eta)/2$ commute with all $U(g)$: the Krein decomposition $H=H_+\oplus H_-$ is **Poincaré-invariant**, hence assigns nothing to any region; every $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal N,\Omega)$-invariant extractable from $(\eta,\mathcal N,\Omega)$ is a global charge/grading datum. In DHR terms, involutive gauge gradings of a Haag-dual split net are superselection/statistics data (Bose–Fermi-type gradings), and the DHR analysis *derives* that structure from the causal-disjointness relation of the index set — i.e. the sliver's signature content is **inherited from the pre-installed causal order $n_1$**, in precisely the dossier-§2 sense of "signature-inherited". `[INFERENCE, medium-high — the DHR framing; the Poincaré-invariance of $P_\pm$ itself is immediate from Step 1]`

**Step 5 (the sliver is non-empty — the certifying example).** In the free scalar net, the Fock parity $\eta=(-1)^N$ is: self-adjoint unitary; commutes with **all** $U(g)$, hence with all $\Delta_W^{it}$ and all $J_W$; fixes $\Omega$; implements the internal $\mathbb Z_2$ ($\phi\mapsto-\phi$), preserving every $M(O)$; is **indefinite** with signature $(\infty,\infty)$ (even/odd Fock grading); is **not** a function of any $\Delta_W$ (it acts within the multiplicity space $K$ of (Q1)); and is in **no** $M(W)$ and no $M(W)'$ (it acts nontrivially on both). So the commutant-to-generated gap **does host objects beyond $W^*(\Delta)$, even fully natural ones** — the honest answer to "can a genuinely new object live there?" is *yes, objects live there* — but Steps 1–4 classify them exhaustively: they are gauge gradings, geometry-void, their indefiniteness indexed by internal charge/statistics, never by a subregion. `[ESTABLISHED — direct computation in Fock space]`

**Conclusion (G-glob).** Under full (R-global) naturality the trichotomy of the conjecture is *proved*: localized $\Rightarrow$ scalar (G-loc, using any one wedge — a fortiori under all wedges); non-localized + algebra-compatible $\Rightarrow$ gauge grading, geometry-void, $n_1$-inherited signature. **Never indefinite and localized.** The iter-10 referee's sliver is treated.

### 3.3 The field reading (G-field): where the residual truly lives

Under (R-field), equivariance constrains the fiber $\eta_x$ only through $\mathrm{Stab}(x)$:

- **Wedge fibers.** $\mathrm{Stab}(W)$ contains the boost $\Lambda_W$; own-flow naturality $[\eta_W,\Delta_W^{it}]=0$ is therefore *forced by naturality itself* (not an extra assumption). Localization ((loc): $\eta_W$ affiliated to $M(O)\subsetneq M(W)$, or even just $\eta_W\in M(W)$) triggers (G-loc): **every wedge fiber is $\pm1$.** The equivariant field $W\mapsto\mathrm{sgn}(\ln\Delta_W)$ ($\eta_0$ as a field) survives exactly because it is localized in *no* $M(O)$ — reconfirming iter-7/9 from a new direction.
- **Conformal double cones.** In a conformal net $\Delta_O$ is geometric (Hislop–Longo / Longo's conformal-net axioms); a double cone is conformally a wedge, and the (G-loc) argument transports (the conformal group supplies one-parameter subgroups with the same scaling relation inside $O$, and the conformal vacuum is unique): double-cone fibers are $\pm1$ too. **For conformal nets, Lemma G closes entirely.** `[INFERENCE, high — transport argument; not spelled out at BW-level rigor this session]`
- **Massive double cones — the residual.** $\mathrm{Stab}(O)\cap\mathcal P_+^\uparrow=SO(d-1)$ (rotations); naturality forces only $[\eta_O,\Delta_O^{it}]=0$ (own, *non-geometric* flow) and rotation invariance. Then $\eta_O\in M(O)\cap\{\Delta_O^{it}\}'=M(O)_\omega$, the centralizer of the vacuum on the double-cone algebra — and here the (G-loc) engine has no fuel: no Borchers translation semigroup maps a bounded $O$ into itself, so $\mathrm{Fix}(\Delta_O^{it})=\mathbb C\Omega$ is not provable by scaling. The existence of a non-scalar fiber is **exactly** the failure of hypothesis $(E_O)$ (vacuum ergodicity on double cones). This is the *entire* analytic residual of the carrier problem after this track.

**Counterexample analysis (what a gap-living carrier must now look like).** `[INFERENCE, high]` Any surviving generative carrier must be: (i) a field over **double cones only** (all wedge fibers scalar — so it has no wedge-level signature, already in tension with reading any Lorentzian metric off it); (ii) each fiber a non-scalar involution in $M(O)_\omega$ — requiring the massive vacuum to have an exact modular symmetry of a bounded region, i.e. $(E_O)$ false; (iii) even then, its per-fiber invariant is (by the iter-10 A2 *referee-corrected* classification, inherited) a **sign pattern resolved per modular eigenspace of $\Delta_O$** — discrete data indexed by the *input* spectrum, choice-dependent, non-metric: $\Delta_{\rm geo}=0$ again; and (iv) assembling fibers into a localized indefinite *field* re-triggers Lemma N2's sign-locus obstruction and the $n_1$ template. The residual is thus **double-walled**: $(E_O)$ (existence wall, `[OPEN]`) and the centralizer-sign vacuity (generativity wall, `[INFERENCE, high]`, inherited-with-correction from iter-10). A counterexample must breach both.
